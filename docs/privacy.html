Privacy information

Applies to iPage Quick GR and NG, versions through 1.0.4. Updated October 4, 2026.

The extension reads the current iPage book's title, author, and ISBN. It sends ISBN, title, or title-and-author searches to Goodreads and NetGalley, visits candidate book pages, and retrieves public book identity, counts, and links. It opens one regular background tab for Goodreads and one for NetGalley, reuses those tabs during the lookup, and leaves them open so you can inspect the source pages. The Close GR/NG tabs control above Notes closes those extension-opened tabs and cancels unfinished lookups. Clicking iPage's selection-list Update also closes those tabs and uses the next-product link as the return address for iPage's normal save, when that link is present. The extension preserves iPage's validation and does not send a separate save request. Moving to another product starts a new lookup and closes the previous product's source tabs. Normal website requests may use your existing signed-in session. These websites receive normal request information such as your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Retrieved results are cached in Firefox's local extension storage. Refresh reruns a lookup; cached entries may remain locally until extension data is removed. The extension does not read the Notes field, extract passwords, summarize reader reviews, or send data to an AI service. It has no analytics or advertising code and no separate research server.

Firefox checks the publisher's configured update host and downloads new signed releases according to Firefox's update settings. The host receives normal web-request information and may maintain access logs; its logging is controlled by the hosting provider and publisher.

Disable the extension in Firefox's Add-ons Manager to stop its research activity. It requests website-content and search-term permissions for the book lookup functions described here.
